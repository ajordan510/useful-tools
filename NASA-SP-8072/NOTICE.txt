Rocket Acoustics Workbench, version 1.0.0

SATURN-related spectrum, source-location and loss equations and the directivity
dataset are adapted from Logan T. Mathews' Scientific Acoustic Tool for
Understanding Rocket Noise, https://github.com/logmath/saturn, commit
7c6d1c3b38ebb32512f704fb281d7516b73fd9d6 (retrieved 2026-10-11).
The original work is licensed under Apache License 2.0; see vendor/SATURN-LICENSE.txt.
Changes: JavaScript stationary implementation, band integration, bounded data
interpolation, validation, energy normalization option, and browser interface.
This application is not SATURN and is not endorsed by its author or by NASA.

SP-8072 table values are approximate manual readings of NASA's public-domain
1971 report, figures 5, 10, 12, 13 and 14. See model-data.json and RESEARCH.md.

Reference-figure excerpts: NASA SP-8072 (Eldred, 1971), Figures 10–13, and
Haynes & Kenny (2009), Modifications to the NASA SP-8072 Distributed Source
Method II for Ares I Lift-off Environment Predictions, Figures 6–7.
These source figures are reproduced as attributed excerpts for explanation
and technical comparison, not as new work or results of this application.
Original figure content is retained; changes are limited to page cropping
and rasterization. See figures/provenance.json for source URLs, pages,
crop bounds and original PDF hashes.
